PRIVACY POLICY · SHORT VERSION
301AD stores your username, your cutoff time, the tasks you type, which apps you allow, and the timestamps of your sessions.
It does not read what is inside those apps, your messages, your files or your location. The lock only checks which app is in front.
Your group sees your name, your cutoff, your declared tasks and your clean-day count. Nobody outside the group sees your tasks.
Delete your account from Me and every row goes with it, same day.
Full text at 301ad.app/privacy. Armenian translation is still in draft.
