301AD · PILOT BUILD
Component set and working prototype
Everything here is drawn as real pixel data on a 220-wide grid and scaled 2x. No vectors, no anti-aliasing, no fractional scaling.
DECIDED FOR YOU
The world is 380px wide, the screen shows 220px of it. The camera pans to follow the character, so the lake and the house are never both fully in frame.
DECIDED FOR YOU
The lockout is reachable two ways: from the state jumper, and by tapping an undeclared app on a stand-in Android launcher.
NEEDS YOUR REVIEW
All Armenian copy is my draft. A native speaker should read the cutoff-missed line and the lockout line before the pilot — the voice rules leave no room for a softened translation.
PLACEHOLDER
The isometric room is a dithered footprint, as you asked. Its fire is real and dims with the session.
